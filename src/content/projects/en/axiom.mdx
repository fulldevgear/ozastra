## Supervising intentions

A list of completed tasks is not enough to understand an autonomous system.
Axiom begins with the goal entrusted to each agent, the decisions it makes and
the conditions that trigger human escalation.

## Showing what deserves attention

The interface separates normal flow from exceptions. It summarizes reasoning
as verifiable decisions and keeps technical detail accessible without imposing
it at the first level.

## Reversible autonomy

Every sensitive action has a checkpoint, a trace and a recovery strategy. The
concept expresses useful applied AI: more autonomous in execution, but never
opaque in its consequences.
