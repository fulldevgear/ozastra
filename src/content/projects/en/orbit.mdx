## Signal before volume

Analytics tools often show everything they know how to measure. Orbit begins
with the opposite question: which information will actually change a decision
today?

The concept organizes the experience around three levels: the state of the
system, signals that deserve attention and available actions. Visualizations
are never decorative; each one answers an explicit question.

## An interface that stays calm

Density is revealed progressively. The first glance provides a stable reading,
then detail appears as the user clarifies their intent. Color communicates
alerts and relationships between data, not spectacle.

## What the concept demonstrates

Orbit is a laboratory for an Ozastra conviction: a B2B product can be
technically powerful without making that complexity permanently visible.
